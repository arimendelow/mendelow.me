# m365crawl render: mendelow.me, the Ouro tools list icon

## Story
A single wooden library card-catalogue drawer, pulled half open, glows warmly from inside because it holds every conversation, ready to be found.

## Camera
85 mm product lens. Eye level: slightly above the drawer, looking down at about 25 degrees, three-quarter view from the front-left so the drawer's front face and its left side both read. One vanishing point, far behind the drawer to the right, where the drawer's side edges and the rows of cards converge.

## Composition
A square icon. The drawer is centred and fills about 80% of the width, with even empty margin on all sides. Nothing touches the edges: the crop band is the whole 1024x1024 frame, and the drawer sits inside rows 140 to 884 and columns 100 to 924. There is no headline or overlay; the safe area outside the drawer stays calm, empty and transparent. The icon must read at 40 px wide, so the silhouette is simple and chunky: one drawer, nothing else.

## Cast
### The drawer
- What: one card-catalogue drawer, the only subject; no people or agents.
- Material: smooth polished honey oak with brass fittings.
- Height: about 45% of the frame's height, including its cards.
- Outfit: none.
- Expression: none; the feeling comes from the glow, a quiet "found it".
- Pose: pulled half open toward the camera, resting level.
- Limbs: none.
- Hands: none.
- Facing: its front face turned three-quarters toward the camera, to the left of frame.

## Props
- The drawer: a short, chunky card-catalogue drawer in warm honey oak, soft rounded edges like a polished toy, the same soft 3D toy-render style as a small wooden factory or desk icon. Its front face carries a small brass label frame (blank, no text) and a round brass pull knob centred below the label.
- The drawer is pulled toward the camera so its open top shows a neat row of cream index cards standing on end, all the same height, front to back.
- One card near the middle stands a little taller than the rest, as if just found.
- The drawer sits flat on nothing: a free-standing object with a soft contact shadow beneath it.

## Setting
Plain, fully transparent background. No room, no cabinet, no shelf, no floor line.

## Light
Soft studio key light from the upper left, and a warm golden glow rising from between the cards inside the open drawer, lighting the cards' top edges and the inside of the drawer walls. A soft contact shadow directly under the drawer seats it.

## Palette
Honey oak, cream card stock, warm brass, and a golden inner glow.

## Must not
- No text, letters, numbers, logos or symbols anywhere; the label frame and every card are blank.
- No figures, hands, snakes, agents or faces.
- No cabinet around the drawer; it is one free-standing drawer.
- No fades, vignettes or background colour.
- No glow halo spilling past the drawer's outline.
- No photographic wood grain noise; smooth, toy-like polished wood.

## Output
Three candidates, each generated whole at 1024x1024 with a transparent background, shipped as 128 px and 256 px WebP icons.
